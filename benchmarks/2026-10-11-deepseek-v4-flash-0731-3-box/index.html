Original V4-Flash vs. 0731, same three boxes, same engine, same test

The Hive Benchmarking Stack (llama-benchy 0.4.0): prompts of 512 and 2,048 tokens, 128-token answers, with no history and with 4,096 tokens of history. Each test runs 3 times. Same engine (b1341), boxes and flags; the change is the model release and its quant (original IQ2XXS 86.7 GB vs. unsloth UD-IQ2_XXS 90.9 GB).
