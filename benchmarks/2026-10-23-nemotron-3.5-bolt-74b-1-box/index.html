Temperatures and power during the run

Sampled every 6 seconds by the hive monitor. The box peaked at 158 °F on both the GPU and the CPU, drawing 118 W at the socket, and settled back to 100 °F within two minutes of finishing. No throttling, no alerts.

What makes this one different

The earlier hive pages pooled two or three boxes to fit one giant model. This is the opposite experiment: the biggest model that lives comfortably on one small box, at a speed you can talk to.

  • Active parameters9B of 74B per word
  • Memory for the model60.6 GB of 80 GB
  • Left over, model loaded~19 GB
  • Requests9 of 9 clean
  • Decode spread, 0 → 16K history1.7%

Room on the box, measured with the model loaded

The GPU can reach 80 GB of the 96 GB: a 48 GB carveout plus 32 GB of system memory the driver lends it. The model takes 60.6 GB of that. The rest is headroom for a draft model, a bigger context, or a second small model.

Linux and the box's own services run in the remaining 16 GB of system memory outside the GPU pool.
